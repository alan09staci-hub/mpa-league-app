1

Join MPA — free, and automatic

Everything starts on the app's front page. Home of the Moultrie Pickleball League at the YMCA in Moultrie, GA — three steps, in order, and the league opens right there.

Cost: $0 ● Live now No approval step
1

DUPR — get your rating account

You need a DUPR account — it's free to sign up, and your DUPR rating is what places you in the right league and seeds you fairly. DUPR is the standard for every MPA league.

If the DUPR signup email won't come through — it happens — you don't get stuck. Rate yourself instead on the 2.5–5.0 picker, right there in Step 1. A self-rating shows everywhere as "Self-rated", never as a DUPR rating, and you can add a real DUPR ID later from My Profile — the flag clears the moment you do.

Try the picker → Self-rated 3.5 is how it displays
2

Become a Member — the free-member form

Name, DUPR ID and rating (or your self-rating), email and phone. That's the form. Membership is automatic — nobody approves you. The moment you submit, you're a member.

Already joined before? "I'm already a member" / Find my name lives inside this step — tap it, find your name, and skip the form. Your email and phone are Admin-only — the organizer can see them, they never appear on any public page.

Cost to become a member: $0 — always
3

How the join itself runs

  • Join Step 1 checks your skill against the band first — before anything else happens.
  • Then it looks up your public DUPR. A match welcomes you back by name — the app already knows you.
  • No match? No problem — you continue as a new free member on the sign-up.
  • Bands are inclusive: a 5.0-and-above band takes 5.0 and up; a 3.5 fits a 3.5–4.49 band, a 4.5 does not.

The one thing that trips people up

Joining creates a pending registration — not a roster spot. You're in line, not in the league, until the organizer adds you. That's deliberate: it's the one human check in the whole journey.

The organizer's side

Your registration lands in Admin → Registrations. The organizer reviews it and adds you to the roster — that's the step that turns a pending registration into a league player.

  • Adding you is what unlocks the league: game days, standings, your dashboard.
  • One person, one record — if you're already a member, there's no duplicate; your DUPR carries over to every league you join.
  • Playing in two leagues? Each league keeps its own standings, misses and penalty — nothing bleeds across.
3

Paying — the league fee

Membership is free forever. A league season has a fee — and the fee is the YMCA's, not MPA's. Here's where the money goes, the round trip paying is designed to take, and the honest status of that design today.

Concept — not live yet Fee set Nov 24, 2026

What the fee is

$20 per league season

The member rate. You pay the YMCA's rate because you're already a YMCA member.

  • Per person, per league — two leagues, two fees.
  • The money goes to the YMCA — MPA doesn't take it. The app only records that it's paid.
  • The organizer can waive a fee per person, per league — and undo it. It's record-keeping in Admin.
  • Membership itself stays $0 either way.

The money goes to the YMCA

When you pay for a league, you pay the YMCA — on the YMCA's own system, ReClique Core (moultrie.recliquecore.com). MPA is never the seller and never holds the money.

  • Where the paying happens on their side is the YMCA's decision. The Pickleball tab in the mockup below is a suggestion — one easy spot for it. The placement is their call.
  • The round trip: tap pay in the app → you leave the app → you pay the YMCA on Core → you come back to the app, where your league registration shows Paid.
  • The app's whole job in this is the last step: recording that it's paid, so the player and the organizer can both see it.

  • Open the proposed Pickleball tab. In this design it sits in the real main nav, after Childcare — flagged “Proposed tab” here because Core has no such tab today, and where the YMCA actually places the paying is their decision.

  • Tap the teal Register button. This is the honest part: it does not open a cart. It takes you straight to the Log In page — “Which best describes you?” — shown in full in the mockup above. Try it: the Register button in the mockup opens that exact view.
  • Sign in, or create an account. Only after that step would Core show whatever checkout follows — and that part cannot be previewed without an account.

Why suggest a tab of its own?

The real nav already carries Programs, Classes, Sports and more. Without a tab for pickleball, each MPA season would sit buried inside a general category where players have to hunt for it. A Pickleball tab would be one tap from the Core front page to every MPA league, and it keeps the league separate from the existing Pickleball Club listing, which is a different program. But it stays a suggestion — if the YMCA would rather put the paying somewhere else on their side, that works too. The placement is theirs to decide; what matters is that the fee is paid to them.

⚠ The honest version, today

There is no MPA league program on Core yet — the only pickleball listing there today is the separate Pickleball Club — so there is no live payment button anywhere a player can see: the fee is hidden from player views on purpose, so nobody is asked to pay into a system that can't take it. And even on the real programs that do exist, Register leads to a Log In page, not a cart — no checkout can be seen, let alone completed, without signing in first. Nothing in the app stops you playing unpaid: the pay-before-you-play gate still has to be built before go-live. So right now the journey is simply: join → get added to the roster → play. Payment is arranged outside the app until the YMCA option exists. And the Paid markers in the return mockups above are part of that same design: the app showing Paid is what the flow is built to do once the YMCA payment option exists — it is not live today. Where on their side the paying happens is likewise still the YMCA's to decide; the Pickleball tab is this design's suggestion, not a settled placement.
