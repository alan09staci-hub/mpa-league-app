01 · Joining

Three Steps to Play

Joining is free and takes a few minutes on a phone. The first page of the app walks a new player through it in order:

  • Get a DUPR account — the national pickleball rating system. If the DUPR sign-up email never arrives, a player can self-rate from 2.5 to 5.0 instead and add a real DUPR later.
  • Become a free MPA member — name and rating go into the member directory. Already a member? There's a "find my name" shortcut in the same box.
  • Join a league — pick the league that fits your rating, and you're on the roster.

League scores are never sent to DUPR — a rating only changes when the member or the organizer updates it.

03 · Game Day

Your Court, Your Games — Nothing Else to Wade Through

On game night, each player sees only their own court and their own games. No scrolling through everybody else's matches.

  • Tap your own name to opt in — your row gets a YOU badge, and the app remembers who you are on that phone.
  • Opt-in closes 3 hours before game time (3:30 PM for a 6:30 PM start). Courts and matches appear once the deadline passes.
  • Courts of 4 to 6 players are set by the standings, and you stay on your court all night.

06 · Standings

Standings That Explain Themselves

Rank is by points earned out of points possible — every player's percentage is right there in the table, along with the week before, wins, losses, and games missed.

  • Columns: Previous rank, Max Movement, E/P Diff %, Penalty, Earned, Against, Possible, Wins, Losses, Played, Missed.
  • Miss penalty ladder: 1st missed night is free · 2nd costs 2.5 points · 3rd costs 5.0 · each one after that costs 2.5 more. It comes off the percentage automatically.
  • Nobody can jump or fall more than 7 spots in a week — the ladder stays a ladder.

07 · The Format

How Scoring Works

60 Points a Night, Every Night

Every court plays for the same 60 points, no matter how many show up. The games just get longer or shorter to fit the court:

4 players

3 games to 20
Everyone plays every game. No byes.

5 players

5 games to 15
One player sits out (a BYE) each game, in turn.

6 players

9 games to 10
Two BYEs each game, in turn.

First to the target score, win by 1. Your standing is points earned ÷ points possible — so a short night and a long night count the same.

09 · One-Off Nights

Round Robin Nights

Separate from the league: one-night round robins with fixed teams that stay together all night. Free round robin nights, or Money Ball nights with an entry fee. Round robin stays open to every member, whatever their rating — like the 50+ league, there is no band to fit.

  • Money Ball: $XX a person — $XX a team. 3rd place gets the team's full entry fee back ($XX), and 1st and 2nd split what's left — 60% / 40%. The entry fee amount is still to be set.
  • Games to 11 — first to 11, win by 1.
  • Once the organizer publishes a night, members join themselves — as a team with a partner, or solo and the organizer pairs them up. Up to 24 teams.
  • Entry fees are paid in person — the app tracks the pot, not the money.
  • No round robin night is scheduled or live yet — it stays in testing until after Tuesday's meeting.

10 · For the Organizer

The Whole League, Run From One Page

The organizer signs in with a passcode and runs everything from the Admin page — no spreadsheets, no paper:

  • Leagues — create and edit leagues; the 8 weekly game days build themselves from the start date.
  • Registrations & members — approve sign-ups onto rosters, edit member details, waive a fee where needed.
  • Game day tools — move a game day (the rest of the season shifts with it), finish a game day, print the court sheet.
  • Announce — email and text drafts written from the league details, ready to copy or send in one tap. Nothing is ever auto-sent.
